# Gas cloud assets

The original procedural cloud geometry and alpha texture generated by
`tools/gas_assets.py`, including their use in `gas.obj`, `BRGTCLOUD.png`,
`bloodwort_spores.obj`, and `BRGBWSP.png`, are dedicated to the public domain
under CC0-1.0. Author: Project Broom contributors, 2026.

No third-party artwork is incorporated. Existing bloodwort and terrain asset
attribution remains in `ANIMATION-LICENSE.md`.
