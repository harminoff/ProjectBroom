# Kobold reptilian skin

Brogue describes the kobold as a lizardlike humanoid of the upper dungeon. Its
catalog glyph is brown (44/33/22 on Brogue's 0–100 color scale), and its attack
verbs are clubs and bashes. Those are the source constraints. Scales, a lighter
plated underside, amber slit eyes, ivory teeth/claws, wood grain and leather
bindings are original artistic interpretations of those constraints, not new
Brogue facts. There is no fur.

The animated kobold now has a dedicated 1024-square diffuse atlas,
`graphics/BRGKOB.png`, generated by `tools/monster_models/kobold_materials.py`.
Eight padded regions separate skin, underside, crest, eye, pupil/mouth, bone,
wood and leather. Existing vertices are remapped into these regions; comparison
against the prior IQM confirms identical positions, normals, indices, weights,
hierarchy, animation samples and bounds. Only UVs and the material reference
change. `BRGM02.png` and the static model/source remain the original reference.

The skin builds with the normal skeletal generator. `skeletal_profiles.json`
records both the active skin and the separate reference skin, so static roster
rebuilds do not overwrite it and the shared renderer review retains the correct
before binding. The Blender source includes the new packed image. Everything is
original Project Broom procedural art under CC-BY-SA-4.0; no outside artwork or
simulation RNG is used. No shaders, texture passes, lights or geometry were added.
The atlas stays at the previous texture resolution.

Verification: 47 relevant resource, skeletal, material and creature tests pass.
Vulkan and OpenGL each render all six clips in a 19-frame review. Background
Blender reopens the source and checks its packed texture, rig and sampled
vertex deformation. Local before/after captures and logs are under
`artifacts/kobold-skin/`; final gallery captures are copied under
`artifacts/kobold-skin/after/`. The atlas and packaged mod regenerate
byte-identically. A packaged OpenGL seed-one dungeon capture exercises the
normal Brogue-controlled presentation.

The engine and launcher source are unchanged, so this asset pass does not repeat
their compilation, installer build or standalone visual comparison. No new
frame-time benchmark was run: geometry, texture resolution and render-pass count
are unchanged. User art approval remains open; this is diffuse shading, without
normal/roughness maps or new dynamic lighting.
